PIKO

A fair-launch, proof-of-on-chain-work token, mined entirely inside canisters on the Internet Computer. No team allocation, no presale, no off-chain server -- the only way any PIKO comes into existence is by paying the same on-chain cost everyone else pays.

Fixed supply, 21M No premine 100% on-chain

This page is the hub, not an app. It doesn't ask you to log in or approve anything -- it just explains PIKO and links out to the two things you can actually do: mine, or play. Both of those live on their own separate canisters below.

Mine it right in your browser -- no wallet software or CLI, just Internet Identity -- or run a self-owned canister that mines around the clock. Every accepted proof costs a small, non-refundable ICP fee: mostly burned permanently, a share converted to cycles to keep the protocol's own canisters funded.

PIKO Dice is a companion game, deliberately hosted on its own separate site with its own login/approval flow -- opting into betting is never bundled with mining. A provably-fair, 1% house edge dice game, resolved by the Internet Computer's own on-chain randomness, giving mined PIKO somewhere to actually be spent.

One token, one ledger, everywhere. The mining site and the dice site move the exact same PIKO through the exact same ledger canister below -- your balance is the same number on both, not separate per-site play money. That's also why this page only ever links to those two applications instead of embedding either: one login, one balance, two things to do with it.

Independent project. Mining is inspired by the publicly described "proof-of-on-chain-work" mechanics of bob.fun, but this is an original codebase, token, and brand -- not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to bob.fun or BOB in any way.
